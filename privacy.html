Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

The short version: Hatch collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers.

What we collect

Nothing.

Hatch has no accounts, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service, and no third party libraries of any kind. The app makes no network requests. There is no server to send data to, because there is no server.

What the app stores on your device

Everything Hatch records stays in a private container on your iPhone:

  • Sessions, when you started a phone free session and how long it lasted.
  • Goals, your daily target, the reward you chose, and whether you met it.
  • Progress, your streak, points, eggs, and the creatures you have collected.
  • Your first name, only if you choose to enter it. It is optional, never required, and you can remove it at any time from the menu.
  • Preferences, appearance, sound, and reminder settings.

This data is shared between the Hatch app and the Hatch Home Screen widget, which is how the widget can start a session and show your progress. It is not shared with anything or anyone else.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Hatch schedules a local notification on your device. Local notifications are created and delivered entirely on your iPhone. No notification service, no push server, and no third party is involved, and no data leaves the device to produce them.

Backups

Because your data lives in the app's container, it is included in your iPhone backups if you have them enabled, either to iCloud or to a computer. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own device settings, not by us. We cannot read them.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate and complete:

  • In the app. Open the menu and choose Erase all data. This deletes every session, goal, egg, creature and preference from your device. It cannot be undone.
  • Delete the app. Removing Hatch removes its container and everything in it.

Because nothing was ever transmitted, there is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and no request you need to send us.

Children

Hatch is suitable for general audiences. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Hatch ever collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than quietly made.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
