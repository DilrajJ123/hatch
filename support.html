Support

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Open a support request on GitHub.

Contact Hatch on GitHub

GitHub issues are public, so please leave out personal details. Including your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Hatch version helps. You will find the Hatch version at the bottom of Settings, General, iPhone Storage, Hatch.

Common questions

When does an egg actually move forward?

On any day you meet your goal. Points and streaks do not affect it. A missed day is stepped over, not counted against you, and the egg never resets.

How do I get the rare creatures?

Longer eggs give better odds. Legendary and Mythic are only possible when every qualifying day on that egg had a target over three hours.

Why did my streak stay the same today?

Today counts once you have met today's goal. Until then the streak stands on yesterday, because today is still winnable.

Can I start a session without opening the app?

Yes. Add the Hatch widget to your Home Screen and tap Start. A running session also appears on your Lock Screen so you can check it without picking the phone up.

How do I delete my data?

Open the menu and choose Erase all data. That removes every session, goal, streak and creature from your device immediately. Deleting the app does the same. Nothing is held anywhere else, because nothing is ever sent anywhere.

I changed my mind about my name

Menu, then Change name. You can remove it entirely from there too.
