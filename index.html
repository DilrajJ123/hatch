Put it down.
Watch it hatch.

Hatch turns time away from your phone into something worth keeping. Set a daily goal. Meet it, and an egg gets one day closer to opening.

How it works

  • Set a goal. Pick how long you want to stay off your phone today, and what you get if you make it.
  • Start a session. Tap the egg, or start one straight from the Home Screen widget without opening the app.
  • Meet the goal. Every qualifying day moves the egg one day closer.
  • Open it. Enough days and the egg hatches into one of 25 creatures. Longer, harder goals make the rare ones possible.

Built to leave you alone

No account. No sign in. No ads. Hatch makes no network requests at all, which means nothing you do in it can be sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Hatch is an independent app for iPhone.
